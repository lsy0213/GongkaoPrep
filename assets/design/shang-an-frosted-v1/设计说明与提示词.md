# 上岸备考 · 简约磨砂玻璃图标

设计日期：2026-10-11。

结合项目现有“打开的书 + 四角文曲星”标志，以及用户提供案例的乳白磨砂玻璃、圆润倒角和柔和灯光。根据用户追加要求“彩色部分不要太多”“尽量简约高级点”，最终将书简化为两片玻璃页，去掉层叠书页、金色描边、彩虹色带和强烈高光。主体接近单色，以乳白与浅灰的明暗表现体积，仅底缘保留很淡的冷色折光。

## 文件

- `shang-an-frosted.png`：1254 × 1254 原始设计稿；浅灰白不透明背景。
- `shang-an-frosted.ico`：从该设计稿转换的 Windows 图标文件，包含 16、24、32、48、64、128、256 像素规格。小尺寸的实际桌面辨识度尚未实机验证。

生成方式：内置 ImageGen。ICO 仅做格式与规格转换，没有另行改变图案。此目录为设计交付，现有应用资源仍保持原有路径。

参考案例仅用于材质和灯光，不使用案例中的品牌图形、文字或水印。

## 最终迭代提示词

输入图为上一轮书与文曲星玻璃图标设计；不是案例中的其他品牌标志。

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Revise the attached app icon according to the user's explicit feedback: MUCH LESS COLOR, MORE MINIMAL, QUIETLY PREMIUM.
Keep the original brand idea: a front-facing open book with one small four-point star above its center inside one rounded-square tile. Preserve this recognizable identity, but actively simplify the design rather than merely recolor the previous shiny render.

SIMPLIFY:
- The book becomes EXACTLY TWO broad smooth page panels, one left and one right, with a simple narrow central fold. DELETE ALL the stacked under-pages, extra book-cover layers, double rims, bright outlines, and decorative contours. One single elegant open-book silhouette, lightly sculpted and softly beveled, like two satin glass slabs.
- The star becomes a small understated FOUR-POINT satin-white star above the book, around 18 percent of tile width. NO gold star, no glowing center, no internal rays, no crystal facets.
- The tile is a simple softly rounded square slab of white frosted glass with a single quiet edge, NO multiple nested borders, NO thick polished crystal rim, NO decorative outlines.

COLOR:
99 percent neutral pearl white / ivory / very pale neutral gray. Remove the blue wash across the whole icon, REMOVE every pink patch and every yellow band and all rainbow stripes. Remove ALL gold edges and glowing highlights. Only one barely perceptible, low-saturation icy-gray-blue refraction at a small portion of the very lowest edge, occupying LESS THAN TWO PERCENT of the entire icon surface. At first glance this is a nearly monochrome white icon. Dimensional separation comes from soft neutral-gray contact shadows, not colorful gradients. Star also white.

STYLE:
Refined minimalist industrial design, milky semi-translucent satin-frosted glass, broad soft white highlights, gentle rounded bevels, balanced empty space. Quiet, understated, simple, luxurious. Almost orthographic straight-on camera. Very subtle depth, not glossy jewelry or a glowing fantasy emblem. Strong simple silhouette readable as a book at small size.

COMPOSITION:
Single centered icon filling about 82 percent of a square 1:1 canvas, wider calm breathing room around the book and star. Uniform opaque off-white #F5F4F1 background with only faint realistic soft shadow below the tile. Smooth pristine outer edges. No transparency, no text, no watermark, no extra symbols, no glitter, no flare, no metallic gold, no bright colors, no rainbow.
```
